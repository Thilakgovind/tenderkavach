# Arun’s second bid

A fictional supplier story illustrated with the live sample workspace.

## Arun opens his workshop

Arun starts a fabrication shop in Coimbatore with two welders and one order book. When a tender asks for steel workbenches, he knows exactly how to make them.

## His first bid falls short

He spends Sunday calculating his price. Weeks later his bid fails technical review. The certificate in his folder expired in March. Nobody ever gets to compare his price.

## A second tender arrives

This time Arun checks the papers before submitting. In TenderKavach he opens Check my bid. Follow along in Sample Mode, using the fictional supplier packet.

## Two documents need attention

Five findings appear. The ISO certificate has expired. The deposit declaration is missing. Another clause needs human review. Arun opens the source before calling his accountant.

## Two calls, two owners

The rescue plan gives each fix an owner. His accountant prepares the declaration. His quality lead looks for the renewed certificate. The app cannot promise when a renewal will arrive.

## Can we mark it passed?

Arun asks if he can mark the missing declaration as passed. The answer is no. He needs supporting evidence. A corrected certificate can clear its own finding while the declaration stays open.

## He knows what is left

Arun downloads the readiness report for his team. He can now decide what still needs fixing before the deadline. The final submission stays in his hands.
